🌾 Why Use AgriSmart Crop Advisory?

AgriSmart organizes crop information in one place so that users can quickly understand crop type, temperature, water requirement, soil conditions and detailed cultivation guidance. The crop records are connected with the MySQL database, allowing authorized administrators to add, edit or remove crop records through the Admin Dashboard.
