Agricultural Policies

Understand major policy areas that influence Indian agriculture, farmer welfare, sustainable farming, irrigation, markets, technology and agricultural development.

Major Agricultural Policy Areas

Agricultural policy covers several areas related to farm production, natural resources, markets, infrastructure, technology and farmer welfare.

🌾

Agricultural Production

Policies and programmes support agricultural productivity, improved technologies, quality planting material and better crop management.

  • Crop productivity
  • Improved technologies
  • Quality inputs
💧

Irrigation & Water Management

Agricultural development includes improving irrigation access and encouraging efficient and sustainable use of water resources.

  • Irrigation development
  • Water-use efficiency
  • Water conservation
🌱

Sustainable Agriculture

Policy initiatives also focus on resource conservation, soil health, climate resilience and sustainable agricultural practices.

  • Soil health
  • Resource conservation
  • Climate resilience
💰

Farmer Welfare

Government programmes provide different forms of support aimed at improving farmer welfare and reducing agricultural risks.

  • Income support
  • Risk management
  • Farmer support programmes
🏪

Agricultural Markets

Agricultural policies also address market access, agricultural marketing, price-related mechanisms and better market participation.

  • Market access
  • Agricultural marketing
  • Price-related mechanisms
💻

Digital Agriculture

Technology and digital platforms are increasingly used for agricultural information, services, monitoring and decision support.

  • Digital services
  • Information access
  • Technology-based agriculture

Policy Focus in Modern Agriculture

Agricultural development increasingly combines productivity, sustainability, technology and farmer welfare.

🌍 Climate-Resilient Farming

Agricultural planning increasingly considers climate variability, efficient resource use and practices that can improve resilience to changing conditions.

🧪 Soil & Nutrient Management

Soil testing, balanced nutrient management and improved soil health are important components of sustainable agricultural development.

🚜 Technology & Mechanisation

Modern agricultural development includes the use of improved machinery, digital technologies and scientific crop-management practices.

👨‍🌾 Farmer-Centric Development

Agricultural programmes also focus on improving access to information, services, infrastructure, markets and other forms of farmer support.
