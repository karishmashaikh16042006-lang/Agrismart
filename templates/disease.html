Crop Disease Advisory

Learn about common crop diseases, their general symptoms, preventive practices and responsible crop protection.

Common Crop Diseases

Early identification and preventive crop management can help reduce disease-related losses. Always confirm the disease before using any plant protection product.

🌾

Wheat Rust

Rust diseases may appear as reddish-brown or yellowish rust-like markings on leaves and plant parts.

  • Monitor crops regularly.
  • Use recommended varieties.
  • Maintain proper crop management.
🌾

Rice Blast

Blast can produce characteristic lesions on leaves and other parts of rice plants.

  • Use healthy planting material.
  • Avoid excessive nitrogen application.
  • Follow local crop protection advice.
🍅

Tomato Leaf Diseases

Tomato plants can be affected by several fungal, bacterial and viral diseases.

  • Remove severely affected plant material.
  • Maintain field sanitation.
  • Avoid unnecessary leaf wetness.
🥔

Potato Late Blight

Late blight can cause dark lesions on leaves and stems and may affect potato tubers.

  • Monitor during favourable weather.
  • Use healthy planting material.
  • Follow locally recommended management.
🌽

Maize Leaf Diseases

Maize may experience different leaf diseases depending on variety, weather and local conditions.

  • Inspect leaves regularly.
  • Maintain field sanitation.
  • Use recommended varieties and practices.
🌿

Soybean Diseases

Soybean can be affected by fungal, bacterial and viral diseases under suitable conditions.

  • Use quality seed.
  • Maintain proper field drainage.
  • Monitor crop growth regularly.

Disease Prevention Practices

Preventive management is an important part of integrated crop protection.

🌱 Before Planting

  • Use healthy and quality seed or planting material.
  • Select suitable varieties for the region.
  • Maintain field sanitation.
  • Follow recommended crop rotation where applicable.

🔍 During Crop Growth

  • Inspect crops regularly.
  • Look for unusual spots, wilting or discoloration.
  • Maintain suitable irrigation practices.
  • Remove and manage affected plant material appropriately.

🧑‍🌾 Integrated Management

  • Combine cultural, biological and other appropriate practices.
  • Use plant protection products only when appropriate.
  • Follow product labels and local recommendations.
  • Use expert advice when disease identification is uncertain.

📋 Regular Monitoring

  • Observe crop health at different growth stages.
  • Record recurring disease problems.
  • Pay attention to weather conditions.
  • Seek agricultural extension advice for serious outbreaks.

⚠️ Important Safety Advisory

AgriSmart provides general educational information and does not replace professional disease diagnosis. Symptoms of different diseases can look similar. Do not select a pesticide or fungicide only from symptoms shown on this website. Confirm the problem and follow the product label, local agricultural recommendations and applicable safety instructions.

🌿 Healthy Crops Start With Early Monitoring

Regular observation, healthy planting material, good field hygiene, balanced crop management and timely expert advice can support better disease management.
